Grid coverage

What share of a fixed lattice of sample points around each city has street-level imagery — an area measure, comparable across providers because both are queried on the same frozen grid. Streets measures the road network instead.

How to read this table

Each city is one row, with a sub-column per provider under every metric, so every provider that collected a city reads across in one line. The Δ column gives one fixed difference (Mapillary − GSV) rather than a moving "best − rest", so its sign means the same thing in every row; a further provider gets its own sub-columns but no Δ. Click any provider's cell to open that provider's snapshot for the city.

Coverage percentages are cross-provider comparable, because every provider is measured against the same frozen grid. Raw panorama counts are not: a sample provider records only the nearest pano at each grid point, so its count is bounded by the grid, while a census provider records every 360° pano in the search area. Each panorama column is labelled with which of the two it is, which is also why that group has no Δ.

Each numeric filter in the sidebar carries a histogram of where the rows actually sit, drawn over everything the other controls have selected, so brushing one range never redraws its own picture. The address bar always carries the view you are looking at so it can be shared.
