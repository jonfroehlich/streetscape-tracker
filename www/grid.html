Grid coverage

Every city in the tracker is sampled on a fixed geographic grid: a lattice of points around the city center, each asked for its nearest street-level panorama. Grid coverage is the share of those sample points with imagery — an area measure, comparable across providers because both are queried on the same frozen grid. The Streets page measures coverage by road network instead: what fraction of a city's streets have actually been driven.

A city collected by both Google Street View and Mapillary appears once per provider. Coverage percentages are cross-provider comparable; raw panorama counts are not — GSV samples the nearest pano per grid point while Mapillary is a census of every 360° pano. Search, filter and re-sort to narrow the table; the strip below the controls shows how the sorted column is distributed across the rows currently in view, and the address bar always carries the view you are looking at so it can be shared.
